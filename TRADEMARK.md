# Trademark policy

The Forge **software** is licensed under Apache-2.0. The Forge and Fornaxlabs
**names and marks** are not.

Apache License 2.0, Section 6 explicitly grants no trademark rights. This page states
what that means in practice, in the spirit of the Linux, Rust and Docker policies.

## You may, without asking
- Use the name "Forge" to refer to this project truthfully — in docs, articles, talks,
  comparisons, or a package that integrates with it.
- Say your project "works with Forge", "is built on Forge", or "is Forge-compatible".
- Redistribute unmodified copies under Apache-2.0, keeping LICENSE and NOTICE intact.

## You may not, without written permission
- Use "Forge" or "Fornaxlabs" in the **name** of your product, service, company, or
  domain in a way that implies endorsement or origin (e.g. "Forge Pro", "Forge Cloud").
- Present a **modified** version as "Forge" — fork freely, but rename it, so users are
  never misled about which enforcement they are actually running. For a tool whose
  value is that its guarantees hold, name confusion is a safety issue, not just a
  branding one.
- Use the marks in a way suggesting Fornaxlabs sponsors or endorses your offering.

## Forks
Forking is welcome and Apache-2.0 permits it. Please give the fork its own name, keep
the NOTICE file, and state plainly that it is derived from Forge.
